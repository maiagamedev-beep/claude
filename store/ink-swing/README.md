# Ink Swing

**Type:** 2D · Grapple platformer · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/ink-swing.zip`

## Description (EN)
Hook, swing and fling a pen-drawn stick hero across 60 notebook levels. Chain swings, bounce on springs, do flips for style and race your best time. Hats and ink colors to collect.

## How to play (EN)
Hold mouse/touch/Space to hook the highlighted ring, release to fly. R restarts the level.

## Descrição (PT)
Enganche, balance e voe com um herói desenhado à caneta por 60 fases de caderno. Encadeie balanços, pule em molas, dê mortais e bata seu tempo. Chapéus e cores de tinta para colecionar.

## Como jogar (PT)
Segure mouse/toque/Espaço para enganchar no anel destacado, solte para voar. R reinicia a fase.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
continue from the last ring, skip level, x3 coins; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
