# Pulse Dash

**Type:** 2D · Rhythm platformer · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/pulse-dash.zip`

## Description (EN)
An auto-running cube over spikes, blocks, jump pads and orbs across 30 levels. Checkpoint practice mode.

## How to play (EN)
Your cube runs by itself. Tap, click or press Space to jump (hold to keep jumping). Avoid spikes, land on blocks, touch yellow pads to launch and tap in the air on rings to double-jump. Turn on checkpoints to practice.

## Descrição (PT)
Um cubo que corre sozinho sobre espinhos, blocos, plataformas e anéis em 30 fases. Modo treino com checkpoints.

## Como jogar (PT)
O cubo corre sozinho. Toque, clique ou aperte Espaço para pular (segure para continuar pulando). Evite espinhos, pouse nos blocos, toque nas plataformas amarelas e toque no ar nos anéis para pulo duplo. Ligue checkpoints para treinar.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
checkpoints, x3 coins; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
