# Noodle Arena

**Type:** 2D · .io arena · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/noodle-arena.zip`

## Description (EN)
Grow the longest noodle in a painted arena full of hungry rivals. Eat pellets, boost past opponents and make them crash into your body. 20 skins, 5 permanent upgrades and a live top-noodles board.

## How to play (EN)
Mouse: steer toward the cursor, hold click to boost. Keyboard: A/D or arrows to turn, Space to boost. Touch: drag anywhere to steer, hold the ⚡ button to boost.

## Descrição (PT)
Cresça o maior macarrão numa arena pintada cheia de rivais famintos. Coma bolinhas, acelere e faça os rivais baterem no seu corpo. 20 visuais, 5 melhorias permanentes e placar ao vivo.

## Como jogar (PT)
Mouse: guie para o cursor, segure o clique para acelerar. Teclado: A/D ou setas para virar, Espaço para acelerar. Toque: arraste para guiar, segure ⚡ para acelerar.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
revive with 90% length, start 3x bigger, x2 coins; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
