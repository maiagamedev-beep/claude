# Bakery Empire

**Type:** 2D · Idle / clicker · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/bakery-empire.zip`

## Description (EN)
Tap the giant bread to bake coins, hire apprentices and build ovens, mills, factories and even a moon oven. Catch the golden croissant for a 7x frenzy, reach x2 milestones and open franchises to earn stars that multiply everything forever.

## How to play (EN)
Click/tap the bread (or Space) to bake. Buy buildings in the shop (x1, x10, Max). Tap the golden croissant when it appears. Franchise to reset for permanent stars.

## Descrição (PT)
Toque no pão gigante para assar moedas, contrate aprendizes e construa fornos, moinhos, fábricas e até um forno lunar. Pegue o croissant dourado para um frenesi 7x, alcance marcos x2 e abra franquias para ganhar estrelas que multiplicam tudo.

## Como jogar (PT)
Clique/toque no pão (ou Espaço) para assar. Compre construções na loja (x1, x10, Máx). Toque no croissant dourado quando aparecer. Franqueie para reiniciar com estrelas permanentes.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
x2 income for 5 min, instant golden frenzy, x3 offline earnings, x3 level-up chest; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
