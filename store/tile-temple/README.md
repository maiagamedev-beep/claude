# Tile Temple

**Type:** 2D · Triple tile match · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/tile-temple.zip`

## Description (EN)
Tap free ivory tiles and collect three of a kind in a 7-slot tray. Layered temple layouts across 60 levels.

## How to play (EN)
Tap tiles that are not covered to move them to the tray. Three identical tiles in the tray disappear. Clear the whole temple before the 7-slot tray fills up.

## Descrição (PT)
Toque nas peças livres e junte três iguais numa bandeja de 7 vagas. Templos em camadas em 60 fases.

## Como jogar (PT)
Toque nas peças que não estão cobertas para levá-las à bandeja. Três peças iguais na bandeja somem. Limpe o templo inteiro antes das 7 vagas encherem.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
take 3 tiles out, free boosters, x3 coins; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
