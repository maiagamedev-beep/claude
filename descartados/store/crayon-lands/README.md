# Crayon Lands

**Type:** 2D · Territory capture · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/crayon-lands.zip`

## Description (EN)
Draw lines out of your land and loop back to claim everything inside — all in wax crayon on sketchbook paper. Cut rival lines, dodge their attacks and reach the target percentage across 40 stages.

## How to play (EN)
Arrows/WASD, swipe, or tap the side you want to turn to. Leave your land to draw, return to capture. Do not let anyone touch your line.

## Descrição (PT)
Desenhe linhas fora da sua terra e volte para conquistar tudo dentro — tudo em giz de cera num caderno. Corte linhas rivais, fuja dos ataques e alcance a porcentagem da meta em 40 fases.

## Como jogar (PT)
Setas/WASD, deslize, ou toque no lado para onde quer virar. Saia da sua terra para desenhar, volte para capturar. Não deixe ninguém tocar sua linha.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
revive keeping your land, x2/x3 coins; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
