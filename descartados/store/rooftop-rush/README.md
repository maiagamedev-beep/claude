# Rooftop Rush

**Type:** 3D · Endless runner · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/rooftop-rush.zip`

## Description (EN)
Sprint down the train tracks of a toy-like town. Switch lanes, jump barriers, roll under bars and run on top of trains. Magnet, jetpack, spring shoes, double coins and boards that save you from a crash.

## How to play (EN)
Swipe or arrows/WASD: left/right change lane, up/Space jump, down roll.

## Descrição (PT)
Corra pelos trilhos de uma cidade de brinquedo. Troque de pista, pule barreiras, role sob barras e corra em cima dos trens. Ímã, jetpack, tênis mola, moedas em dobro e skates que te salvam de uma batida.

## Como jogar (PT)
Deslize ou setas/WASD: esquerda/direita troca de pista, cima/Espaço pula, baixo rola.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
revive, +2 boards, x2 coins; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
