# Lantern Night

**Type:** 2D · Survivor roguelite · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/lantern-night.zip`

## Description (EN)
Carry your lantern through a night of shadow creatures. Weapons fire on their own — collect light sparks, level up and build your kit from 6 weapons and 6 blessings. Survive 10 minutes until dawn on 4 stages with 4 heroes.

## How to play (EN)
Move with WASD/arrows, or drag anywhere on touch. Pick an upgrade when you level up. Esc pauses.

## Descrição (PT)
Leve sua lanterna por uma noite de criaturas das sombras. As armas atiram sozinhas — pegue faíscas de luz, suba de nível e monte seu kit com 6 armas e 6 bênçãos. Sobreviva 10 minutos até o amanhecer em 4 fases com 4 heróis.

## Como jogar (PT)
Mova com WASD/setas ou arraste na tela. Escolha uma melhoria ao subir de nível. Esc pausa.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
revive, reroll upgrade choices (3 per run), x2/x3 coins; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
