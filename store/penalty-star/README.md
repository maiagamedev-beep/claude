# Penalty Star

**Type:** 2D · Football penalties · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/penalty-star.zip`

## Description (EN)
Swipe to shoot with curve, tap to dive and save. Win a 3-round knockout cup against fictional clubs.

## How to play (EN)
Shooting: swipe from the ball toward the goal — faster swipes shoot harder, curved swipes bend the ball. Saving: tap where you want the keeper to dive before the ball arrives. Keyboard: arrows/Q/E aim or dive. Win 3 knockout matches to lift the cup.

## Descrição (PT)
Deslize para chutar com curva, toque para defender. Vença uma copa mata-mata de 3 fases contra clubes fictícios.

## Como jogar (PT)
Chutar: deslize da bola em direção ao gol — mais rápido chuta mais forte, deslizar em curva faz a bola curvar. Defender: toque onde o goleiro deve pular antes da bola chegar. Teclado: setas/Q/E miram ou pulam. Vença 3 jogos para levantar a taça.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
retake last kick, x3 coins; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
