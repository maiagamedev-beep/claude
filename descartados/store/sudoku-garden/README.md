# Sudoku Garden

**Type:** 2D · Logic puzzle · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/sudoku-garden.zip`

## Description (EN)
Generated sudoku with unique solutions in four difficulties, notes, hints, mistakes and a daily puzzle.

## How to play (EN)
Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the numbers 1 to 9 without repeating. Tap a cell, then a number. Use notes (N) for candidates, Z to undo. Three mistakes and the round ends.

## Descrição (PT)
Sudoku gerado com solução única em quatro dificuldades, anotações, dicas, erros e desafio do dia.

## Como jogar (PT)
Preencha cada linha, coluna e quadrado 3×3 com os números de 1 a 9 sem repetir. Toque numa casa e depois num número. Use anotações (N), Z desfaz. Três erros e a rodada acaba.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
hints, keep playing after 3 mistakes, x3 coins; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
