# Hop Street

**Type:** 3D · Road crosser · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/hop-street.zip`

## Description (EN)
Hop a voxel critter across busy roads, rivers full of logs and train tracks. How far can you go before the hawk catches up? Unlock 11 characters from the prize machine.

## How to play (EN)
Tap or Up/W/Space to hop forward. Swipe or arrows to hop left, right and back.

## Descrição (PT)
Pule com um bichinho voxel por ruas movimentadas, rios com troncos e trilhos de trem. Até onde você chega antes do gavião? Desbloqueie 11 personagens na máquina de prêmios.

## Como jogar (PT)
Toque ou Cima/W/Espaço para pular para frente. Deslize ou setas para os lados e para trás.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
revive, free prize machine spin, x2 coins; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
