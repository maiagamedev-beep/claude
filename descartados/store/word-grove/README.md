# Word Grove

**Type:** 2D · Word search · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/word-grove.zip`

## Description (EN)
Themed word search in English or Portuguese — 60 puzzles that grow from 7×7 to 12×12 with all 8 directions.

## How to play (EN)
Find every word in the list. Press on the first letter and drag to the last one — words can go across, down or diagonally, forwards or backwards.

## Descrição (PT)
Caça-palavras temático em inglês ou português — 60 desafios de 7×7 a 12×12 em todas as 8 direções.

## Como jogar (PT)
Encontre todas as palavras da lista. Pressione a primeira letra e arraste até a última — as palavras podem estar na horizontal, vertical ou diagonal, em qualquer sentido.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
hints, x3 coins; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
