# Deep Mine Tycoon

**Type:** 2D · Idle tycoon · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/deep-mine.zip`

## Description (EN)
Dig ore, run the elevator and sell it. Upgrade shafts, hire managers, open deeper shafts and new mines.

## How to play (EN)
Tap the miners, the elevator and the cart to move ore up and sell it. Upgrade each level to produce and carry more. Hire managers to automate them. Dig new shafts deeper for richer ore and open new mines to multiply everything.

## Descrição (PT)
Extraia minério, opere o elevador e venda. Melhore poços, contrate gerentes, abra poços mais fundos e novas minas.

## Como jogar (PT)
Toque nos mineiros, no elevador e no carrinho para levar o minério e vender. Melhore cada nível para produzir e carregar mais. Contrate gerentes para automatizar. Abra poços mais fundos e novas minas para multiplicar tudo.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
x2 cash for 10 min, free managers, x3 offline earnings; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
