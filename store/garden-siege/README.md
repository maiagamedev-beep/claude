# Garden Siege

**Type:** 2D · Tower defense · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/garden-siege.zip`

## Description (EN)
Plant peas, thorn bushes, frost mint, puffball mortars, sunflower beams and bee hives to stop waves of ants, beetles, wasps, snails, caterpillars and giant stag beetles. 30 stages in cut-paper style, 3 upgrade tiers per plant and a greenhouse of permanent bonuses.

## How to play (EN)
Tap the grass to plant, tap a plant to upgrade/sell. ▶ or Space calls the next wave (early = bonus seeds). Keys 1-6 pick plants, Esc cancels. 1x/2x/3x speed.

## Descrição (PT)
Plante ervilheiras, espinheiros, hortelã gelada, cogumelos-bomba, girassóis e colmeias para parar ondas de formigas, besouros, vespas, caracóis, lagartas e besouros gigantes. 30 fases em estilo papel recortado, 3 níveis de melhoria por planta e estufa de bônus permanentes.

## Como jogar (PT)
Toque na grama para plantar, toque numa planta para melhorar/vender. ▶ ou Espaço chama a próxima onda (antes = sementes bônus). Teclas 1-6 escolhem plantas, Esc cancela. Velocidade 1x/2x/3x.

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
+150 seeds, continue with +10 hearts, x2/x3 coins; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
