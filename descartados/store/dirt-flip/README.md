# Dirt Flip

**Type:** 2D · Bike physics · **Mobile:** yes (touch controls) · **Made with AI:** yes · **Upload:** `dist/dirt-flip.zip`

## Description (EN)
Ride a dirt bike over 30 generated tracks with ramps, bumps and hills. Land flips to cut your time.

## How to play (EN)
Up/W = gas, Down/S = brake, Left/Right (A/D) = lean back/forward (spin in the air). On touch use the four buttons. Land on your wheels — flips take 1.5 s off your time!

## Descrição (PT)
Pilote uma moto de trilha em 30 pistas com rampas, lombadas e morros. Acerte mortais para reduzir o tempo.

## Como jogar (PT)
Cima/W = acelerar, Baixo/S = frear, Esquerda/Direita (A/D) = inclinar (girar no ar). No toque use os quatro botões. Caia sobre as rodas — mortais tiram 1,5 s do seu tempo!

## Rewarded video placements (always optional)
checkpoint revive, x3 coins; plus the shared meta systems below.

Every game also has a daily login calendar, a lucky wheel (free every 4h + optional video spins), a free gift every 8 minutes, 3 daily missions with rerolls, player level with chests and cloud save.

Interstitials (`midgame`) are only requested after a game over / level end, when the player presses the restart or next button, and never more than once every ~2 minutes.
